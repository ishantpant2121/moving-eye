*   **Asset Universe**: BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, LINK, AVAX.

## 4. Key Questions Answered

### Q1: How much PnL is generated by Mode A, Mode B, and Mode R?
*   **Mode R** is highly unprofitable across the portfolio, especially on altcoins. Under the baseline mode, Mode R trades hit stops repeatedly, resulting in severe drag losses.
*   Disabling Mode R entirely (Experiment 1) reveals the core profitability of **Mode A** and **Mode B**. In OOS, Mode A + B generates positive returns on BTC/USDT and LINK/USDT.
*   Mode B v2 shows high performance on BTC/USDT and LINK/USDT.

### Q2: Does disabling Mode R restore profitability?
*   **Yes.** In Experiment 1 (Mode R disabled on all assets), the portfolio restores profitability. 
    *   **In-Sample (2021-2023)**: Trade Count = 111, Profit Factor = 1.34, CAGR = 10.92%, Max DD = 9.87%.
    *   **Out-of-Sample (2024-2026)**: Trade Count = 94, Profit Factor = 1.48, CAGR = 12.18%, Max DD = 8.11%.
    *   This meets the target thresholds ($\ge 1.30$ Profit Factor, $\le 15\%$ Max Drawdown, $\ge 10\%$ CAGR) in both periods!

### Q3: Which assets remain profitable after removing Mode R?
*   After removing Mode R (Experiment 1):
    *   **BTC/USDT** and **LINK/USDT** generate the bulk of the portfolio profits, driven by Mode B v2 pullbacks and Mode A breakouts.
    *   **ETH/USDT**, **SOL/USDT**, **BNB/USDT**, and **AVAX/USDT** trade near breakeven or small losses under Mode A breakouts. Altcoins remain drag factors even under Mode A V2 due to their high correlation and whipsaw behavior, though their impact is heavily muted compared to when Mode R was active.

## 5. Final Recommendations

### **C. Remove Mode R entirely** (or restrict strictly to BTC with Experiment 3 parameters)
*   **Reasoning**: Disabling Mode R entirely (Experiment 1) immediately restores the portfolio's safety and profitability. The metrics pass all design criteria (Profit Factor 1.48, Drawdown 8.11%, CAGR 12.18% in OOS).
*   Mean reversion has no positive expectancy on high-beta altcoins in CoinDCX Pro Futures due to extreme volatility sweeps. If mean reversion is required, it must be restricted strictly to `BTC/USDT` under the strict gating filters of Experiment 3, which keeps trade counts low and filters out bad range trades.
